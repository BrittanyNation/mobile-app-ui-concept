Mobile App UI Concept · Portfolio Project

A calmer way to plan a homeschool day.

NestDay is a fictional homeschool planner app concept designed for parents who are teaching at the kitchen table, not managing a classroom. It keeps today’s lessons, the week ahead, attendance, and work samples in one gentle place — no red badges, no pressure, just a clear next step.

Who it is for

🏠

The homeschool parent

Teaching one to three children at different levels, planning in short pockets of time, and needing the day to be visible at a glance.

📋

The record-keeper

Tracking attendance days and collecting work samples for a portfolio review, without turning record-keeping into a second job.

🌱

The gentle planner

Preferring encouragement over streaks and alarms — progress shown softly, unfinished lessons simply moved, never marked as failures.

The screens

Each screen is presented inside a phone frame and built from the same components — cards, progress bars, chips, toggles, and one bottom tab bar — so the system, not just the screens, is the design.

Design notes

Palette — soft sage & warm cream

Green is the color of a kitchen plant and a quiet morning. Sage carries the primary actions, warm cream keeps long planning sessions easy on the eyes, and a muted gold marks celebration moments only. All text is dark ink on light surfaces for safe contrast.

Type — readable first

A warm serif for gallery headings gives the concept its editorial, homey feel. Inside the app, a clean system sans at generous sizes keeps lesson titles scannable one-handed, with small caps labels for structure.

Components — one kit, every screen

Cards, chips, progress bars, list rows, toggles, and the tab bar repeat unchanged across all seven screens. A parent learns the pattern once; every new screen already feels familiar.

About this project

NestDay is an original portfolio concept by Brittany Nation, created to demonstrate mobile UI systems, screen flows, and consistent component design. Her design workflow runs in Sketch and Webflow; this demo is hand-built in HTML and CSS so the screens can be explored live in a browser. NestDay is a fictional brand — no affiliation with any real app, company, or curriculum is claimed.
